# Publication information

Chapter 7 — The Internal Factory of Language Models

English publication v1.0 · 4 October 2026

Seven reports form a sequence from internal motion and discrete output, through training objectives and computational handoff, to routing, repair and comparisons across architectures. The chapter opens with a CoT trajectory replay and the generation-factory display. A source-position ledger accompanies the displays.

The release contains 209 rendered Word pages, 136 embedded figures, 71 report tables and 652 numerical measurement CSVs from 49 experiment packages. The Word files match the prepared English report editions. Each report keeps its original experiment identifiers.

The full publication archive contains the reports, figures, evidence and displays. The evidence archive contains numerical measurements, figures, published table transcriptions and their indexes. The interactive archive contains three standalone HTML views and an entry page; unzip it and open `index.html`.
