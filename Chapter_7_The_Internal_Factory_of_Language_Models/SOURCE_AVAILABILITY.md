# Source availability and measurement scope

[Chapter 7](README.md) · [Evidence index](EVIDENCE_INDEX.md)

The English reports were prepared from the consolidated model-introspection report v0.19, its living record v3.1 and the report-specific experiment archives dated 2–4 October 2026. Original experiment identifiers are retained. The seven Word reports are the previously prepared editions; their text and embedded figures are unchanged in this chapter release.

This release includes the report text, all 136 embedded figures, all 71 published tables, an experiment map and the available numerical measurement CSVs. The evidence manifest specifies the archive, row count, column count and SHA-256 for each numerical file. Published table CSVs are separately identified as transcriptions. An experiment appearing in the consolidated report does not imply that a separate runnable experiment archive is present here.

External source datasets, original prompt text, character-level source content, model checkpoints and the private editorial workspace are outside the public package. Measurement columns remain available. Where a CSV carried source text alongside measurements, only the listed text columns were withheld; numeric values were not rounded or recalculated. Controlled symbolic tokens used in the CoT arithmetic task remain readable.

The two front-page demonstrations use different measurement units. The CoT page replays 344 recorded transitions from eight controlled 10-dimensional recurrent trajectories. The generation-factory page displays 18 internal frames for each of six sentinel cases in an eight-layer, four-head, 24-dimensional controlled classifier. It follows assembly before a single TEXT/ACT output. The screen projection helps display the measurements; operational motion labels derive from the original 24-dimensional measurements.

The CoT interface is a new publication view over the frozen CSV records. The generation-factory interface retains the archived motion display and numerical data, with English labels and neutral source-position labels in place of source text. The provenance ledger likewise exposes positions and measured routing states. These are recorded-data displays, not live model inference. No experimental training or measurement was rerun for this publication.

Report-specific evidence and inference should be read within the stated task, model and intervention conditions. Architecture comparisons, local causal interventions and broader design interpretations remain distinct in the reports.
