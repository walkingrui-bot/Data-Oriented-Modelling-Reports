## Reading the final decision

EXP001–010 support the existence and inspectability of the coordination mechanism under the stated constructions. EXP011–019 establish the role of native statistics, calibration and out-of-fold routing, with bounded shared-layer increments. EXP020–088 separately test source identification, ordinary statistical models, additional channels, capacity, optimization and transport. EXP089 is a retrospective engineering synthesis, not a new pooled experiment.

The real-task decision retains task-specific statistical baselines and leaves additional Stat-MoE or shared-ganglion investment dormant until new identifiable, independent data justify it. This does not invalidate the controlled mechanism or establish that sharing fails universally.

## Numerical provenance note

The four domain-level improvements in [EXP048 METRICS.json](evidence/EXP048/METRICS.json) range from 10.036% to 22.273%, consistent with the report’s rounded 10.0%–22.3%. The original EXP089 matrix lists 10.19% as its lower bound; that is the same-station validation gain, not the minimum across all four domains. The primary metrics govern this comparison. The archived matrix remains identifiable as the original secondary summary.

Selected final-report quantities were checked directly against their aggregate files; [verification record](VERIFICATION.md) documents the scope.
