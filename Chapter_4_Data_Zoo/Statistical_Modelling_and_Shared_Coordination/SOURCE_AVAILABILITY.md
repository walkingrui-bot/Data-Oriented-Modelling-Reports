# Source availability

The source report, supplied closeout files and complete original materials archives are preserved separately. This release distributes authored research assets and a combined English report; it does not redistribute external observation datasets.

Included materials comprise research code, original protocols and amendments, result and gate reports, aggregate metric files, optimization traces, author-generated figures, the 18-pipeline passport design and controlled simulator assets for EXP001–003. These first three experiments generate their observations from specified synthetic mechanisms. They are explicitly identified as simulations.

Excluded materials comprise external source observations, cached or joined source tables, row-level transformed features, labels, observation-linked predictions, participant/split assignments and sample-level audit products. Real-data binary checkpoints and raw execution transcripts are also kept outside this public snapshot because they have not been exhaustively cleared of source-dependent content. No Parquet tables or real-source NPY/NPZ arrays are distributed. The exclusion inventory lists the individual source-relative paths and hashes without reproducing their contents.

EXP004 uses a real measurement table. EXP005–010 combine a real numerical backbone with controlled measurement transformations; the resulting observations are not recast as independently collected natural modalities. EXP011–089 use the source-specific qualifications stated in their records. Source qualification, feature construction and predictive validation remain different claims.

Provider URLs are retained in SOURCE_REFERENCES.csv and the experiment protocols. Reproduction of real-data experiments requires separate authorized retrieval of the specified source versions under their provider terms. A current download is not a substitute for a historical data version, and later access to a source does not establish earlier public availability.

PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_MANIFEST.csv records the source and public hashes. Research content is preserved in its original working language. Relative links are adjusted for the public directory structure, and links to excluded assets are labelled as references outside this snapshot. EXP069 and EXP071 aggregate OOF metric derivatives omit participant-level fold ID assignments; all aggregate metric values are retained. Original versions remain in the source archive.

The original EXP001 standalone evidence archive was recovered independently during publication preparation, filling the absence noted in the older handoff. The programme now has a public evidence entry for every identifier from EXP001 to EXP089. SOURCE_ARCHIVES.json records the original archive hashes. Completeness of this index does not imply that every original file is publicly redistributed.

Local source roots and incidental application/theme metadata are normalized in the public copies. Publication references use canonical identifiers or the original dataset citation page where appropriate. These changes do not alter experimental values; source hashes remain in the manifest.
