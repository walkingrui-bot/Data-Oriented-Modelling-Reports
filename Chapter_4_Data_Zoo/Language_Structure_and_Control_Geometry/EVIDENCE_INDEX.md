## Report extracts

The 79 CSV files in `evidence/report_tables/` are exact transcriptions of source research and evidence tables. They are not raw corpus files or new calculations. The source version-history table is process metadata and is excluded. The 69 images in `figures/` retain the original embedded image bytes. `FIGURE_MAP.csv` records source labels, publication numbers, source blocks, hashes and any identical recovered study image. `TABLE_MAP.csv` gives the corresponding table mapping.

Figure filenames inside historical study folders follow their source numbering and can differ from the publication order. Use the publication maps to identify the correct image and caption.

The four studies without recovered independent archives are LSP-002, LSP-003, TOOL-CHAIN-GEOMETRY-006B and NATLANG-COMPAT-004A. Their source-report claims are retained with availability annotations.
