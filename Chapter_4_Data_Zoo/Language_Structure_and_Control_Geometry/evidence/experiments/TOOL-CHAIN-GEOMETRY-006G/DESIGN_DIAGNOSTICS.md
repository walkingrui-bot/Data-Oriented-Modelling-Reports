# TOOL-CHAIN-GEOMETRY-006G design diagnostics

1. The formal planner was not evaluated until it passed an independent seen-schema competence gate: 91.96% oracle-prefix exact action, 72.92% canonical final-state accuracy and 73.33% seen-raw final-state accuracy.
2. Six raw schema families were represented by distinct reserved result-value coordinates. Only styles 0-2 were active during training; styles 3-5 were reserved but never activated, making the raw test a genuine held-out input-support intervention.
3. Canonical normalization was defined upstream of planner input. The same hidden backend schema can therefore produce either a held-out raw representation or the trained canonical representation without changing task identity or returned numeric value.
4. The data-geometry precheck was executed before interpreting behavior. It confirmed that raw seen/unseen prefixes are strongly separated, whereas their canonicalized counterparts are pairwise identical.
5. Canonical seen/unseen prefixes are exact duplicates by construction. TwoNN estimates on the duplicated union are degenerate and are not used as evidence. Stable/participation/PCA spectra are retained as descriptive profiles; the decisive shift check is the matched zero displacement after normalization.
6. The formal campaign uses the same three 200-task held-out world panels as 006D-006F and crosses them with seen vs unseen schema regimes, yielding 4,800 executed trajectories.
7. All three held-out raw styles were also evaluated separately. Each produces the same qualitative collapse, ruling out a single-format explanation within this controlled schema family.
