The publication carries authored derived evidence and source identities. External/provider observations, source audio, mel tensors, participant-level gait records and provider archive snapshots are outside the distributed files. Source access and reuse follow the respective providers’ terms.

The speech summaries refer to two held-out recordings partitioned into 103 segments. The shared scaler and segment resampling describe that specimen. The gait geometry refers to four equally weighted subgroup-mean displacement vectors; its coordinate permutations form a conditional matrix reference distribution. These units determine the scope of the reported comparisons.

The source evidence describes a higher-precision working gait matrix and distributes a three-decimal public matrix. Report-recorded values remain alongside the reproducible public-matrix results. The two precision levels are identified in report Table C and the reproduction guide.

Three nonsignificant ALC cells were cross-checked against primary Table 2, page 28: command-and-control repetitions; spontaneous word interruptions; and command-and-control word interruptions. The side-by-side mapping in report Table B supports use of the primary labels while retaining the original study transcription.
