# Language–Movement Integrated Study

Data-Oriented Modelling · Chapter 4 Data Zoo · Subreport 03 · English edition v1.0 · 29 September 2026

[Data Zoo](https://github.com/walkingrui-bot/Data-Oriented-Modelling-Reports/tree/main/Chapter_4_Data_Zoo) · [All chapters](https://github.com/walkingrui-bot/Data-Oriented-Modelling-Reports)

A compact perturbation can unfold into a richer observable trajectory. This study examines that possibility through language–movement reference geometry, task-conditioned speech calibration, acoustic temporal geometry, and controlled-drinking gait summaries. It separates the geometry of a condition-related displacement from the local dynamics through which an output system realizes change.

The Word edition has 26 pages, no headers or footers, all 13 supplied figures, and all 12 scientific/evidence tables. Three added tables provide terminology, primary-source verification and public-matrix reproduction. The opening summary, reading guide, self-contained captions and practical checklist accompany the full scientific argument.

The acoustic study describes 103 segments from two source recordings. The gait study analyzes four subgroup-mean displacement vectors derived from a 100-participant source study. The cross-domain integration is a working hypothesis about how a perturbation is realized across different output systems.

The public three-decimal gait matrix has been rerun with the supplied script and 200,000 feature-identity permutations. Its results agree with all nine supplied rounded-matrix reference quantities. Original report precision and the public-matrix rerun are labelled separately. Three ALC calibration cells have a primary-source mapping annotation in report Table B and in the evidence directory; the study’s original numeric table is retained.

The collection contains authored derived matrices, summaries, figures, analysis scripts and source identities. Provider recordings, tensors and participant observations are obtained from their original distribution channels.
