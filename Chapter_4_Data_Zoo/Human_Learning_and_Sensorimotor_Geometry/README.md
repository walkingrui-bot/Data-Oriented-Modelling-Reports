# Human Learning and Sensorimotor Geometry

Biosphere 3 · Chapter 4 Data Zoo · Subreport 2 · English edition v1.0 · 27 September 2026

[Data Zoo](../README.md) · [All chapters](https://github.com/walkingrui-bot/biosphere-3-public-reports)

Four studies connect the geometry of recorded data, human learning behavior, whole-body movement, and reaching with muscle activity. Together they distinguish the number of recorded coordinates, the breadth of recruited effectors, and the concentration of temporal variation. The report develops a shared sensorimotor interpretation from these separate measurements.
