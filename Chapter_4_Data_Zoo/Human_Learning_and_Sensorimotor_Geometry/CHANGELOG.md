# Version history

## English edition v1.0 · 27 September 2026

Combined Studies 001–004 into one report with a continuous section sequence, 21 numbered study tables and 17 figures. The edition adds an executive summary, research map, terminology, reader notes and self-contained source notes. Original scientific statements, values, tables and images are retained; process announcements and prospective handoff plans are removed from the reading edition.

The online report mirrors the approved 31-page Word edition. The publication collects authored analysis tables, figures and scripts, an evidence index, source references, image and table maps, and integrity records. The Word edition uses plain formatting with no headers or footers.

The source retrieval utility uses Git's NUL-delimited blob header to verify pinned inputs, supports study selection, and reuses verified local files. External inputs are identified by source references for separate retrieval from their providers. The public package contains the merged reading edition and derived evidence; provider datasets and original source attachments are outside its scope.
