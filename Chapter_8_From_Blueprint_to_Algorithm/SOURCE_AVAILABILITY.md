# Source availability

[Chapter 8](README.md) · [Evidence index](EVIDENCE_INDEX.md)

The English edition is based on the final Chinese manuscript **From Blueprint to Algorithm**, dated 6 October 2026, supplied as `BLUEPRINT_NEURAL_ALGORITHM_DECOMPILATION_FINAL_REVIEW_20261006(1).docx`.

Source manuscript SHA-256: `bc95338f20d749f3f8a16e96ddf3f05c150e0f7ad056742e79f3487dcef28421`.

The publication retains its experiments, numerical results, examples and substantive discussion. It adds the Chapter 8 introduction and maintenance policy, explains terms, specifies evaluation conditions beside the results, and removes writing-process statements and discussion of a separate prospective architecture. Section 12.2 presents the source comparison matrix as readable comparisons; the matrix is also supplied as CSV. The repeated closing display of Figure 9 is replaced by a reference to the same figure.

The nine English figures reproduce the source diagrams or recorded measurements with English labels. Original images are retained in `evidence/source_figures`. The numeric plots have companion CSV files. Figure 6 uses a zero baseline for percentage bars; the original used a truncated vertical range. Values are unchanged. No confidence intervals were invented.

The evidence manifest records both the source and published SHA-256 for every archived file. Experiment reports, numeric records and five synthetic-task checkpoints are unchanged. The EXP001 script omits one non-experimental environment-identification comment; its executable code is unchanged. No external raw dataset is included.

The original manuscript and experimental records support the reported results. The publication checks confirm transcription, figure values, file integrity and navigation. They do not constitute an independent experimental replication. Availability limits for the preliminary EXP003 test and the flexible-order model parameter count are recorded in the evidence index.

All additions remain scoped to this explanatory sequence. Future evidence will be maintained in the corresponding topical chapter and linked from this synthesis.
