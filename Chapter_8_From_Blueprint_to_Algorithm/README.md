## How neural networks generate answers

The earlier chapters followed internal motion, reasoning, assembly and control. This chapter brings their evidence together and asks what computation actually generates an answer. Controlled experiments identify plans that can be transplanted to new problems, recover explicit algorithms from trained networks, and show how intermediate states, output order and training history shape execution.

**This is the synthesis and conclusion of the current research sequence.** Its evidence has now been assembled into a relatively detailed account of answer generation. Future findings and remaining gaps will be added to the corresponding topical chapters and linked back here. Direct results are identified for the controlled systems in which they were measured; the connections to larger language models retain their stated evidential scope.
