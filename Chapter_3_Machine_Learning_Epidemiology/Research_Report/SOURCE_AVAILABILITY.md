# Source availability

The collection has two complementary evidence layers. The original experiments and model-scale study include archived data, scripts, figures and the checkpoints available in their source packages. The two subsequent replication campaigns are represented by their supplied completed reports, review, audit and chapter-coverage map.

[REPLICATION_SOURCE_MAP.csv](REPLICATION_SOURCE_MAP.csv) lists each locally referenced campaign file in context. `included_source_report` identifies an attached source available here. `referenced_by_replication_report` identifies a protocol, raw JSON, log, checkpoint or other supporting file cited by a completed report as a companion record outside the included file inventory. Reported replication results retain attribution to their campaign report. These availability labels describe the assembled files separately from the reported experiment outcomes.

The original review records a 37-archive, 985-member snapshot of the preceding edition. This integrated edition includes the added scale archive and new records. Current counts are in [EVIDENCE_INDEX.md](EVIDENCE_INDEX.md).

[REFERENCED_FILES.csv](REFERENCED_FILES.csv) covers the two historical Word branches. [EVIDENCE_MANIFEST.csv](EVIDENCE_MANIFEST.csv) records included evidence and hashes. [SOURCE_PROVENANCE.csv](SOURCE_PROVENANCE.csv) preserves the identities of the six supplied Markdown sources.
