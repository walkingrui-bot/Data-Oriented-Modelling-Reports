# E39 Residual future influence


In pure noise, the band changes from 13–111 to 13–45 against a true near-optimal interval of 20–54. Jaccard rises from 0.354 to 0.619 and the fraction of clearly bad time points inside the band falls from 73.5% to 0%. Bands in the other two worlds remain unchanged.

## Method

Keep E38’s worlds and thresholds and add residual FCI/external anchor FCI<5%. The constructed world permits latent-noise interventions, making future influence outside the three attributed sources measurable. Apply the same rule to all three worlds.

## Final interpretation

GCM combines source attribution and residual monitoring. The residual experiment intervenes on a latent-noise channel; the attributed FCI values measure each specified source intervention. Their interactions form a further experimental target.
