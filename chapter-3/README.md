## Evidence and interpretation

The research component includes 38 original experiment archives and two completed replication/validation campaigns represented by their supplied reports, reviews, and audits. The engineering component includes nine cloud experiment directories, 229 preserved cloud archive members, eleven original stage bundles, and the supplied local reports. Stage bundles can overlap chronologically and do not denote eleven independent replications.

The [review resolution](REVIEW_RESOLUTION.md) records the final description changes. [Snapshot inventory](SNAPSHOT_INVENTORY.json) records mechanically checked quantities. The [combined description](ZENODO_DESCRIPTION_EN.md) provides the publication text.

Information readable from an internal state and information causally used by the output are distinguished by intervention evidence. The scaling study measures separation and local first-order sensitivity in a controlled 400-case world. Its proposed connection to functional interference and capability transitions is a hypothesis for causal testing. The local 135M patch result is a selected-case intervention; the 100-task selector and forty-task paraphrase results have separate denominators.

Each component’s source map distinguishes included files from referenced companion records. Deterministic release checks re-evaluate the included numerical evidence and reported arithmetic. They do not constitute a new training or pretrained-inference run.
