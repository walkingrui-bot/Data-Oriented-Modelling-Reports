# Biosphere 3 — Project Information

Biosphere 3 is a personal-interest research project by an independent researcher. It began with curiosity about what language models do as they generate, reason, and respond to their own outputs.

The work is self-directed and conducted without academic supervision or institutional research guidance. Its questions, experiments, and interpretations are developed independently by the author.

## A living body of work

The project is published as an evolving collection of research findings, experiments, and ideas. Each release brings together the current conclusions and the evidence behind them. New experiments can extend the account, sharpen its predictions, or change its direction.

The published findings and guides are in English. Original experimental materials retain their source language and format. Experiment IDs connect the prose to methods, results, code, tables, and figures.

## Versions and discussion

Tagged releases preserve particular stages of the work. The change history records new results and changes in interpretation, so readers can follow the ideas as they develop.

Questions, replications, alternative explanations, and new experimental ideas are welcome. Include the release version and experiment ID when discussing a specific result.
