# 009 — Matched marginals and distinct transition families

Synthetic experiment.

Two persistent hidden families share the stationary N(0,I) marginal and observation basis but differ in their transition rotations. Current-frame and two-frame classifiers audit family information. Recurrent shared, learned-router, transition-cluster and oracle-router models are compared with a single-frame predictor.

Read [report section 5.7](../../../REPORT_EN.md#57-geometry-matched-mechanism-families-009-similar-marginals-and-different-transitions) for the full methods, comparisons and interpretation.
