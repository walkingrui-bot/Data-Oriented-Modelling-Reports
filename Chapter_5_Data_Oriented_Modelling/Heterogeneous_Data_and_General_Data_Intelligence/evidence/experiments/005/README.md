# 005 — Observability and history

Synthetic experiment.

Full and partial observation are crossed with one or four hidden laws. A single-frame predictor, a four-frame recurrent predictor and a recurrent mixture are compared. The observability calculation evaluates the specified finite history: the partial single-frame rank is four and the four-frame rank is eight under the tested dynamics.

Read [report section 5.3](../../../REPORT_EN.md#53-world-observability-005-history-and-hidden-state-reconstruction) for the full methods, comparisons and interpretation.
