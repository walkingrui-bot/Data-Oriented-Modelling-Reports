# 004 — Curvature, innovation and anisotropy

Synthetic experiment.

Latent dimension 8 and observation dimension 24 are fixed while curvature, additive innovation and anisotropy vary across eight conditions. The archive measures local response geometry and compares shared and routed predictors, including capacity and quadrant-oracle interventions. Final-step rollout MSE is distinct from the mean rollout metric.

Read [report section 5.2](../../../REPORT_EN.md#52-world-data-microstructure-004-curvature-noise-and-anisotropy-at-fixed-dimension) for the full methods, comparisons and interpretation.
