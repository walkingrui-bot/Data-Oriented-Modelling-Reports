# 017 — History and delayed resource depletion

Synthetic experiment.

Observable availability responds to an unobserved cumulative wear reservoir after a delayed threshold. HistoryAware and AnticipatoryCoordinator share a recurrent architecture but differ in whether training propagates gradients through resource dynamics and includes reserve costs. Wear probes and a matched-current-state history intervention assess the retained resource information.

Read [report section 7.3](../../../REPORT_EN.md#73-anticipatory-coordination-017-routing-under-delayed-resource-depletion) for the full methods, comparisons and interpretation.
