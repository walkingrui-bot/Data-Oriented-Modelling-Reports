# 014 — Informative measurement policies

Synthetic experiment.

The same latent trajectories are observed under severity-dependent, weakly dependent and denser scheduled measurement policies. Value-only, mask-and-time, auxiliary-policy and multi-policy strategies are compared. Matched repeated-exposure and permuted-mask controls help identify the source of gains. Counterfactual policy swaps hold the latent world fixed.

Read [report section 6.5](../../../REPORT_EN.md#65-informative-sampling-policy-014-when-missingness-is-generated-by-the-world) for the full methods, comparisons and interpretation.
