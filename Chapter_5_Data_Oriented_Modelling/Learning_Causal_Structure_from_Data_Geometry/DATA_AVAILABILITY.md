# Data availability

No external provider raw measurements are included in this release. The supplied Sachs/CORNETO and Tübingen analyses are represented by research outputs, protocols, code, source links and provenance. Source observations must be obtained independently from the provider. Acquisition scripts are included as source code and were not executed during publication preparation.

Real-data completion/trace NPZ files are excluded because their arrays may retain observed inputs alongside predictions. The exclusion does not remove aggregate findings from the report. Model parameters, aggregate losses and geometries, masks, row-selection indices and preprocessing summaries remain available. The 600-world and three-variable synthetic arrays are research-generated experimental material and are included.

CG-001–002 and the external pilot lack separately recovered execution records. CG-003–008 are partial. CG-014–016 have protocols and result summaries but no recovered execution scripts. Later source scripts may reference original local runtime paths and preceding experiment assets; those paths must be configured for a new run. The availability index identifies these distinctions.

Sachs source provenance is recorded in CG017/CG018 source_provenance.json, including provider URLs, acquisition date and SHA-256. Tübingen/CausalDiscoveryToolbox sources are referenced in the corresponding analysis scripts and the report bibliography. The REAL45 files under CG023 are previously completed aggregate comparison results, not new training or provider datasets.
