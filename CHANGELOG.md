### Current interpretation

- Dynamic generative rule fitting is the unifying working hypothesis; RTG is a constructed mechanism within that framework.
- Future influence, horizon, readability, control gain, and residual influence are distinct measurements.
- Reference identification and stabilization are separate tasks; mirrors and direct center estimation are parallel implementations.

- Balance Band describes specified source dynamics; a measured residual channel extends source coverage.
